# Phase 5 toy counterexamples

All 35 preregistered settings and five seeds are retained: 175 datasets, 350 Bayes/ERM evaluation rows. Metrics and fitted parameters are numerically replayable. See summary.json and paired_contrasts.json for complete seed-level results and intervals.

Symmetry alone does not imply hard prediction concentration. At zero information with uniform priors, deterministic label tie-breaking and random tie-breaking can yield different hard prediction concentrations under the same posterior. Positive temperature changes posterior confidence without changing score ordering. Unequal priors or feature variances are distribution assumptions, not a universal noise law. Linear ERM and Bayes are separate baselines; these toys do not establish the mechanism of any radio dataset or neural checkpoint.
